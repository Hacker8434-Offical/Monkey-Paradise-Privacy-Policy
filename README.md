# Monkey Paradise Discord Bot – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Monkey Paradise Discord Bot (“the Bot,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the Bot or use its features within the Monkey Paradise Minecraft server community.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain it as required by applicable law.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide and maintain its features, the Bot may collect and process the following information, depending on the features enabled.

### Discord Information

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Channel IDs and Role IDs
- Message IDs and Interaction IDs, where necessary
- Server configuration and settings
- Verification and moderation settings
- Logging and ticket configurations
- Role and permission configurations

### Minecraft Account Information

If Minecraft-related features are enabled, the Bot may collect or store:

- Minecraft usernames
- Minecraft UUIDs
- Linked Discord and Minecraft account information
- Account verification status and timestamps
- Whitelist status and related records
- Minecraft server access and eligibility information
- Minecraft-related moderation records, where applicable

This information may be used to associate a Discord account with a Minecraft account and provide server-related functionality.

### Verification Data

If verification features are enabled, the Bot may store:

- Verification status
- Verification timestamps
- Verification logs
- Account-linking records
- Relevant verification attempts and results

### Moderation Data

The Bot may store moderation-related information, including:

- Warnings
- Mutes and other restrictions
- Kicks and bans
- Staff notes
- Appeals, if enabled
- Moderation reasons and timestamps
- Relevant Discord or Minecraft identifiers

This information may be used to enforce community rules, maintain moderation history, and prevent users from evading punishments.

### Ticket and Support Data

If ticket features are enabled, the Bot may store:

- Ticket IDs
- Ticket creators and participants
- Ticket messages or transcripts
- Ticket status and timestamps
- Staff responses and internal notes
- Information voluntarily provided when requesting support

Ticket information may be accessible to authorized staff responsible for handling the relevant request.

### Command Usage and Diagnostic Data

To maintain reliability and security, the Bot may record:

- Commands used and their execution status
- Relevant user and server identifiers
- Error and diagnostic logs
- Usage statistics
- Security-related events
- Information necessary to investigate bugs or abuse

The information recorded depends on the Bot's implementation and configuration.

## 2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The Bot is not designed to collect the following information as part of its ordinary operation:

- Discord or Minecraft account passwords
- Credit card or banking information
- Government-issued identification documents
- Email addresses or phone numbers, unless a specifically disclosed feature requires them
- Private Discord messages that the Bot has not been permitted or configured to access

The Bot may process message content or other information when necessary for enabled features, such as moderation, ticket transcripts, message logging, or support requests.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary sensitive personal information. Please avoid submitting sensitive information through Bot commands, tickets, or community channels.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by the Bot may be used to:

- Operate and maintain Bot features
- Support the Monkey Paradise Minecraft server
- Verify and link Discord and Minecraft accounts
- Manage whitelisting and server access
- Provide moderation and administrative tools
- Process support tickets and appeals
- Maintain moderation and audit records
- Prevent spam, abuse, and unauthorized access
- Investigate violations of community rules
- Troubleshoot errors and improve reliability
- Maintain the security and integrity of the Bot and its integrations

Information is not intended to be used for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for commercial purposes.

Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

**Server administration:** Authorized Monkey Paradise staff may access relevant information through moderation tools, verification systems, ticket transcripts, and audit logs.

**Security and abuse prevention:** Relevant information may be reviewed when investigating suspected abuse, security incidents, ban evasion, or violations of community rules.

**Third-party services:** Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the Bot, such as hosting providers, databases, or external APIs, where applicable.

**Legal obligations:** Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or in response to valid legal requests.

**User requests:** Information may be shared when necessary to fulfill a request made by the relevant user or when otherwise lawfully authorized.

Access to information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the features being used.

Information may be retained to:

- Operate Bot features
- Maintain verification and account-linking records
- Preserve moderation history
- Prevent abuse and ban evasion
- Resolve support requests and disputes
- Maintain system security
- Meet applicable legal obligations

Where practical and appropriate, information may be deleted when it is no longer required or following a valid deletion request.

Some records may be retained for a reasonable period when necessary for security, moderation, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Deleting a Discord or Minecraft account does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of every record associated with that account.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Bot against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the Bot's infrastructure, these measures may include access restrictions, permission controls, and appropriate safeguards for stored information.

However, no internet-based service, database, or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should not share passwords, authentication tokens, or other confidential credentials through the Bot or its associated community.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with other services necessary for its functionality.

These may include:

- Discord
- Minecraft-related services and server software
- Hosting and database providers
- External APIs
- Other integrations enabled by Monkey Paradise

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Monkey Paradise does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Users should review the relevant policies when using those services.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically directed toward children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord under applicable requirements.

Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements and comply with any other applicable age-related restrictions.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from users in violation of applicable age-related privacy laws.

If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, please contact Monkey Paradise staff so the matter can be reviewed.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Request information about personal data associated with you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of personal information where applicable
- Ask how your information is collected and used
- Object to or request restrictions on certain processing where applicable
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal limitations. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate security, moderation, abuse-prevention, or legal purposes.

To make a privacy request, contact the Monkey Paradise staff through the official Discord server. Do not submit passwords or other confidential credentials as part of your request.

## 10. Responsibilities of Server Administrators

Server administrators are responsible for configuring and using the Bot appropriately within their Discord servers and Minecraft communities.

Their responsibilities may include:

- Selecting which Bot features to enable
- Configuring moderation and logging systems
- Managing access to tickets and administrative records
- Informing community members about relevant logging practices
- Handling collected information responsibly
- Ensuring their use of the Bot complies with applicable laws and platform policies

The information available to administrators depends on the permissions granted to the Bot and the features enabled.

## 11. Cookies and Website Tracking

The Bot itself does not use browser cookies as part of its ordinary Discord command functionality.

If Monkey Paradise provides a separate website, dashboard, verification page, or other web-based service, that service may use cookies or similar technologies if necessary. Any applicable tracking or cookie practices should be explained in a separate notice where appropriate.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in Bot functionality, data practices, security measures, or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published through an appropriate Monkey Paradise community channel or other designated location.

The effective date will be updated when appropriate. Where required by law, users will be notified of material changes or asked to provide consent.

Continued use of the Bot after an updated policy takes effect constitutes acknowledgment of the updated policy where permitted by applicable law.

## 13. Contact and Privacy Requests

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, contact the Monkey Paradise staff through the official Monkey Paradise Discord server.

Please provide enough information for staff to identify and review your request without sharing unnecessary personal or confidential information.
